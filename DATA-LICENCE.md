## Bank B is excluded, deliberately

`banks/bank_b_locators.json` contains **no item text** — only source file,
directive identifier, option count, key index, and a SHA-256 of each item.

Bank B's items come from a textbook licensed under the **GNU Free Documentation
License 1.3 with Invariant Sections**. Those terms bind distribution of copies
and modified versions. Measuring over the items distributes nothing; publishing
them here would. The locators plus the hashes make the measurement reproducible
without redistribution.

The same reasoning excludes `data/runs/generate_bankBS_ol.jsonl` and its
quarantined predecessor: tutor replies to Bank B items sometimes reproduce
option text verbatim.
